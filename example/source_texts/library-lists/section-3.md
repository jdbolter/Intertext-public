[harley-1989] Harley, "Deconstructing the Map" (1989). Harley urged students of cartography to begin from the premise that cartography is seldom what cartographers say it is (p. 1), and to look for the social forces that have structured it and for the presence of power in all map knowledge (p. 2). Use: the critical view that a map's surface hides whose interests it serves. Rely on no more than these two points.
