[borges-1946] Borges, "On Exactitude in Science" (1946). A one-paragraph fiction, first published in 1946 and attributed to an invented seventeenth-century author. In an empire where cartography has reached such perfection that the cartographers make a map the size of the empire, matching it point for point, later generations find that map useless and leave its ruins in the western deserts. Use: the joke that a map which kept everything would answer no question. No pages are given for it.
